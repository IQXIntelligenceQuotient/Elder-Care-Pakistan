Donor trust

Give through verified, official donation channels

Your support helps Elder Care Pakistan respond to verified needs among older people and their families. Donations are applied according to approved programme needs, accepted restrictions, and the organisation's donation policy.

Use only bank information published or issued through official Elder Care Pakistan channels. We do not request donations through personal bank accounts and we will never ask for your online banking password, card PIN, one-time password, or personal banking login.

Important donation notice

International donations

Donate by International Bank Transfer

Supporters outside Pakistan may donate through an international SWIFT bank transfer. Register your intended donation to receive verified transfer instructions, a unique donation reference, and guidance for submitting transfer evidence.

  1. Submit the international donation request.
  2. Receive verified SWIFT transfer instructions.
  3. Make the transfer through your bank.
  4. Include the issued donation reference.
  5. Submit the transfer reference or receipt.
  6. Receive confirmation after bank reconciliation.

Notice: Uploading a bank transfer receipt does not itself confirm that funds have been received. Elder Care Pakistan confirms a donation only after the amount is credited to and verified in its authorised organisational bank account.

Tax: Tax treatment depends on the laws of the donor's country and the donation channel used. Elder Care Pakistan does not represent that a direct donation to Pakistan is tax-deductible outside Pakistan. Donors should obtain independent advice where necessary.
