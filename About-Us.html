About Us

Our values

Elder Care Pakistan helps older people and their families find practical support while respecting their dignity and independence.

Explore care support

  • Respect

    Listen to older people and respect their experience, choices, and independence.

  • Compassion

    Respond with kindness and pay attention to the needs of each person and family.

  • Responsibility

    Confirm programme capacity before promising support and verify information before publishing it.

Leadership Messages

Guided by Compassion, Built on Responsibility

Elder Care Pakistan is driven by a sincere commitment to protect elderly lives with dignity, care, comfort, and community support.

Founder's Vision Care, dignity, and lifelong support for elders.
Founder's Message

Give a Helping Hand for Old Age

"Live for others is called life."

Dear friends and supporters, our mission is to serve humanity, especially our elderly, with compassion, justice, and values rooted in faith.

In a world that often overlooks seniors, Elder Care Pakistan honors the wisdom, sacrifice, and legacy of our elders. We envision a modern care complex where elderly people live with dignity, comfort, safety, and emotional support.

Together, we can change how society views aging and help seniors live with reliable care, trusted information, social connection, and respect.

Faiz Alam Faizi

Founder, Elder Care Pakistan

Executive Director's Message

A Vision of Dignity and Care for Our Elderly

"Embracing our elders with love, dignity, and care."

It is a privilege to serve a mission rooted in compassion, respect, and dedication to the well-being of our elderly community.

At a time when modern life often leaves seniors isolated, Elder Care Pakistan chooses to honor and uplift them through structured care, medical support, and community responsibility.

With your support, we can build a future where every elder feels valued, supported, connected, and protected.

M. Imran Khan Rana

Executive Director, Elder Care Pakistan

Donor answer

Why donors support the Elder Care Pakistan mission

The mission is simple: help older people live with dignity, safety, and compassionate support when families need trusted elder-care guidance.

Your gift strengthens practical care for seniors in Pakistan, including medical coordination, mobility support, safer homes, and essential assistance for elders with verified needs.
