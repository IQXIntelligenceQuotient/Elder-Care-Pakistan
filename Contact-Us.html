Donor trust

Give through verified, official donation channels

Your support helps Elder Care Pakistan respond to verified needs among older people and their families. Donations are applied according to approved programme needs, accepted restrictions, and the organisation's donation policy.

Use only bank information published or issued through official Elder Care Pakistan channels. We do not request donations through personal bank accounts and we will never ask for your online banking password, card PIN, one-time password, or personal banking login.

Important donation notice

Uploading or sharing a bank transfer receipt does not itself confirm that funds have been received. A donation is confirmed only after the amount is credited to and verified in the authorised organisational bank account.

Report suspicious requests
Donor answer

Ask before you donate, then give with confidence

The contact page helps donors, families, and partners ask questions before giving, especially when they need verified instructions for money, QR, SWIFT, or gifts in kind.

Clear communication protects donors and helps Elder Care Pakistan guide support toward needs that can be verified, accepted, and used responsibly.

Donor FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Learn how to give, what your donation supports, and how we confirm your payment.

Can I contact Elder Care Pakistan before donating?

Yes. You can send a message, call, or use WhatsApp to ask for verified donation guidance before giving.

Where should donation questions be sent?

Donation questions can be sent to eldercarepakistan@gmail.com or through the official WhatsApp contact listed on the site.

Should I share bank passwords or OTPs?

No. Elder Care Pakistan does not need banking passwords, card PINs, OTPs, or personal banking logins to receive a donation.

Need help donating?

Our team can guide you through the official payment options.
